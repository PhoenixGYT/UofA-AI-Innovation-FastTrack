Guide 1: HR Onboarding Basics

First day start time

On day 1, new hires start at 9:00 AM local time. Join the onboarding chat 10 minutes early to test audio.

Welcome call and first-week schedule

The welcome call is at 10:30 AM on day 1. Your full first-week schedule is emailed by HR before 5:00 PM on your first day.

Requesting time off

Request time off in the People Portal under Time Off. Submit requests at least 10 calendar days in advance and include your coverage plan.

Approval flow for leave

Your direct manager reviews time-off requests. HR can answer policy questions, but only your manager approves or denies requests.

Guide 2: Support Team Practice Playbook

Practice support replies

Use the Sandbox Inbox for your first week. Draft replies to sample tickets before sending anything to customers.

Feedback process

Post draft replies in the #support-coaching channel. Your assigned mentor reviews drafts twice daily and gives feedback.

Working in the support inbox

For live tickets, first tag urgency, then check macros, then write a clear and empathetic response. Escalate billing or legal issues to a lead.

Guide 3: IT Setup and Security

Laptop setup

Turn on your laptop, connect to internet, run Company Setup Assistant, and install security updates before opening work apps.

Sign-in and multi-factor authentication

Sign in with your company email and temporary password, then set up multi-factor authentication in the Authenticator app during first sign-in.

IT support contact

If your laptop is not working, contact IT Help in #it-help or email it-help@company.example. Include a screenshot and device serial number.

Security policy

Never share one-time codes, passwords, or recovery codes with anyone, including teammates and managers.

Guide 4: Demo Onboarding Handbook

30-60-90 onboarding plan

During your first 30 days, focus on product basics and shadowing. By day 60, handle standard tickets independently. By day 90, own a weekly queue block with mentor spot-checks.

Daily standup expectations

Post your standup update by 9:30 AM in #support-standup using: yesterday, today, blockers. Tag your mentor if a blocker is unresolved after one business day.

Escalation response times

Escalate urgent customer-impacting issues within 15 minutes to the on-call lead. Non-urgent escalations should be documented in the ticket within the same business day.

Communication quality checklist

Before sending customer replies, verify tone, confirm next steps, and include a clear owner and timeline. Keep messages concise and avoid internal jargon.
